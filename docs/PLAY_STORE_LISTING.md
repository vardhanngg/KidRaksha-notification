# Google Play listing — working draft

## App name
KidSuraksha

## Short description
Transparent notification sharing for parents, with visible child-device controls.

## Full description
KidSuraksha helps a parent stay informed about notifications shared from a paired Android child device. The child device explicitly enables Android Notification Access, optionally enables message-content sharing, and shows a persistent KidSuraksha status notification while sharing is active.

KidSuraksha is built for transparent parental monitoring—not secret monitoring. Parents can pair and revoke devices, review notification history, manage retention, export account data and delete the account.

## Important disclosure text
KidSuraksha can access notifications from other apps only after Android Notification Access is enabled. Message content is optional and may contain private information. Android may redact notification content, and KidSuraksha does not bypass Android privacy protections.

## Claims to avoid
- Do not claim hidden or stealth monitoring.
- Do not claim access to content Android has redacted.
- Do not claim access to camera, microphone, location, calls, SMS or contacts when those features are not part of the final build.
- Do not imply that the app can monitor another adult without their knowledge or consent.

The final listing text must be reconciled with the actual Play build and legal review.
